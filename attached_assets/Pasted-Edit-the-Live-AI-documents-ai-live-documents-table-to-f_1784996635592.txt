Where to Find Your Advertorial (Media Mavens)
Members often look for the advertorial (presell page) inside the Media Mavens platform. It is not there. The Media Mavens offer card contains your unique affiliate link and the product's sales page URL — not the advertorial.

The pre-built advertorials live inside the Flexy website templates:

The Cutting Edge — used for gadget offers

Log in to Flexy (launch it from Tools & Apps > Apps (/apps) in the portal for auto-login).
Go to Sites in the left navigation, then select Websites (not Funnels).
Open the template folder that matches your offer type (Consumer Watchdog or The Cutting Edge).
Clone the latest template — the advertorial body copy comes built into the cloned pages.
Once cloned, open the advertorial page to read it — this is what you use to research your ad angles and headlines. Keep the body copy as provided; you'll test headlines and hero images on top of it.
