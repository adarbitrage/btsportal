## Key Facts

**Platform doctrine — Taboola/Realize (official):**
- Single focal point + tight crop on people is core doctrine: "People are always preferable — even better if they're at medium zoom from the shoulders up, or even closer." "Avoid image clutter. Clean images with a single center of focus will help you stand out." (https://www.taboola.com/help/en/articles/3878032-programmatic-creative-best-practices, Apr 27 2025)
- Taboola's own Top-5 Visual Best Practices: "Include People… Focus on shoulders up"; "Keep it real and clear: Authentic and unpolished visuals with a clear focus point"; "Include hands holding" products (faces perform best as standalone visuals); use eye-catching colors. (Creative Best Practices 2025 PDF)
- **Concrete specs (Taboola):** Static image 1200×674 px, 16:9, JPEG preferred, 5 MB max ("image will auto-crop unless you adjust manually — recommended"); headline 35–45 chars (60 max); description 180–200 chars (250 max); branding text 30 chars max. Display follows layout principle "image > text > logo" with "minimal text." Thumbnail min 600×400 px, 2.5 MB max. (Creative Best Practices 2025 PDF; thumbnail policy Apr 2025)
- **Text-on-image + safe zones (Taboola Native Stream Guidelines):** "We highly recommend that no ad copy or text appear in the image… Because Taboola has multiple ad sizes and dynamically adjusts the image to fit." If text/logos are used, keep them in a **300 px safe zone on each side** (native stream) or within a **467 px center safe zone** (single static image); non-safe areas may be cropped per device. "Images should lean towards simplicity over fine, small details." Avoid black-and-white (blends into feed). Static image 1067×600 px, ratios 16:9 preferred/2:1/4:3/6:5/7:5. (https://s.yimg.com/cv/apiv2/adspecs/format/demos/542/NEW_Taboola_-_Native_Stream_Image_Guidelines__1708425977401.pdf)
- **Content policy relevant to health/ClickBank angles:** Thumbnails must not be misleading and **must not show before/after photos** (explicit example: weight-loss supplement). (https://www.taboola.com/help/en/articles/3878208-title-thumbnail-and-branding-text-policies, Apr 2025)

**Platform doctrine — Outbrain (official specs):**
- **Standard Native:** PNG/JPG **1200×800 px**, ≤2.5 MB; title 100 char max (60 recommended); description 150 char max. Carousel and App Install use **1:1 ratio** (300×300 display). (https://www.outbrain.com/help/ads-specs/)
- Note: Outbrain's public "headline & image best practices" tips live inside an infographic image (not machine-readable); the text page only frames the claim. Treat specific Outbrain image tips as Tier-3/unverified from that source.

**Columbia AI-vs-human study (separate 2026 report, NOT in the April-2025 PDF):**
- Led by Columbia Business School Vice Dean Oded Netzer (with Harvard, TU Munich, Carnegie Mellon), using Taboola/Realize data. Scale: **300K+ ads, 3M+ clicks, 500M+ impressions.** Finding: AI-generated creative performs **on par with, and frequently outperforms, human-made creative**; the headline mechanism is that AI "wins by appearing authentically human and prioritizing visual trust" — i.e., quality/perceived authenticity of the message matters more than who created it. (https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/press/ai-ads-match-human-creative-major-report-columbia-university-harvard-university; https://realize.com/columbia-realize-study-report/). ⚠️ The specific per-driver CTR data ("red flags that kill CTR") is gated behind a lead-capture download; I could not extract the granular numbers.

**Visual complexity at small size (Tier-1 academic):**
- Eye-tracking study (108 participants, 90 usable): **low-complexity banner ads outperformed high-complexity** — noticed only 0.84% faster but fixated 9.09% less, viewed 4.79% less often, and rated 4.40% less appealing. Complex ads demanded more cognitive effort, were more distracting, and contributed to banner blindness. (https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/13/24/13317, 2023)
- Foundational reference: Pieters, Wedel & Batra, "The Stopping Power of Advertising: Measures and Effects of Visual Complexity," *Journal of Marketing* 2010 — distinguishes feature complexity (hurts attention) from design complexity (can help). (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1509/jmkg.74.5.048)

**UGC / candid-vs-polished — TRIANGULATED beyond vendor blogs (mixed, nuanced):**
- UGC digital-trust meta-analysis: 128 effects, 19 platforms, 19 yrs (2004–2022). Consumers trust UGC more than marketing comms *because* it signals authenticity — BUT **content-enhancing features like photo filters weaken UGC's effect on firm performance** by prompting suspicion of misrepresentation; account verification strengthens it. This directly supports "keep it real/unpolished" but grounds it in trust, not novelty. (Hochstein, Harmeling & Perko, *J. Acad. Marketing Science* 53(4):1034-1054, 2025; https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/joamsc/v53y2025i4d10.1007_s11747-023-00982-y.html)
- Influencer meta-analysis: 71 papers/135 studies/571 effect sizes — influencers beat brand posts, virtual influencers, and celebrities, working **indirectly via credibility and attractiveness**. (Springer JAMS 2025, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11747-025-01107-3)

## Notable Claims Requiring Cross-Reference

- **"Candid/UGC beats polished stock" is NOT cleanly supported — it's context-dependent.** A controlled experiment (same brand/product, UGC vs professional) found **professional advertising generated significantly HIGHER trust and purchase intention, and UGC was NOT rated more authentic** (~80 respondents; authenticity→trust→purchase-intention chain). (GUPEA, Univ. of Gothenburg, https://gupea.ub.gu.se/items/d55dcd01-9602-4de7-a8b2-14e114b9ca24/full). RECOMMENDATION for the training series: reframe from "UGC wins" to **"perceived authenticity that earns trust wins"** — polished-but-authentic can beat sloppy-UGC; the real lever is trust, and *over-processing (filters) erodes it* (meta-analysis). This retires the vendor-folklore framing and replaces it with defensible mechanism.
- "Faces perform best standalone; hands-holding for products" — single-source (Taboola PDF), plausible but vendor best-practice, not independently verified.
- Descriptions "can increase performance by up to 8%" — single-source Taboola Native Stream PDF; vendor stat, uncited methodology.
- Columbia study's granular CTR drivers — cited by Columbia (Tier-1 institution) but the numeric detail is only in a gated Taboola-hosted PDF; treat specific figures as Tier-2/promotional until the peer-reviewed paper is public.
