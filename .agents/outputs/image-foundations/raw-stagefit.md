Research complete. The stage-dependence framework is well supported by academic + industry sources.

## Key Facts
- **Congruence increases processing fluency → better attitudes.** Picture–text congruence in web design boosts product attitude *via* processing fluency (mediated), per an experimental study using an online hotel booking site (Journal of Interactive Marketing, AMA). Effect held for consumers high in need-for-cognition — grounds the "creative and page must symbolically match" claim (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intmar.2009.10.003).
- **Picture–word consistency drives elaborative processing.** Classic Houston, Childers & Heckler (1987) study: consistency between an ad's picture and verbal claim affects how deeply the message is elaborated and recalled — foundational academic root for image/headline match (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/002224378702400403).
- **"Information scent" = user's imperfect estimate of value from a source's cues; it is *relative to the need*.** NN/g defines it within information-foraging theory: users pick the path with the highest scent, and scent must remain consistent across steps or users backtrack/bounce. NN/g explicitly has a section "Why Click Bait Doesn't Work in the Long Run" — directly supports the funnel-continuity thesis (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/information-scent/, Feb 2020).
- **Post-click experience (dwell time, bounce rate) is a valid ad-quality proxy, and bad post-click hurts future clicks.** Yahoo Gemini native-ads paper (Lehmann/Lalmas, KDD 2015): negative post-click experience reduces user engagement AND future ad clicks; they built a landing-page-quality model to demote low-dwell ads. This is the strongest evidence that curiosity-maximizing creatives producing junk clicks are penalized downstream (http://janette-lehmann.de/docs/pub2015_kdd.pdf).
- **Clickbait features have mixed/asymmetric effects on engagement.** PLOS ONE study (Jung, Stieglitz et al., 2022; 4,400+ Facebook posts): some clickbait features (unusual punctuation, common clickbait phrases) increase interaction while *others decrease* engagement — clickbait is not uniformly positive, supporting "bounded/qualifying curiosity" over raw curiosity maximization (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9242456/).
- **Source credibility (expertise, trustworthiness, homophily) drives consumer behavior — meta-analytically.** Ismagilova, Slade, Rana & Dwivedi (2020), Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services, meta-analysis of 20 papers (405+ citations): source expertise, trustworthiness, and homophily significantly affect consumer behavior. Proper replacement for the weak hospitality-SME blog source flagged in the prior report (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretconser.2019.01.005).
- **Ohanian source-credibility scale lineage.** Ohanian (1990, Journal of Advertising) constructed/validated the 3-dimension scale — expertise, trustworthiness, attractiveness (https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00913367.1990.10673191); Ohanian (1991, Journal of Advertising Research, 1,255 citations) links perceived spokesperson image to purchase intention (https://doi.org/10.1080/00218499.1991.12466759). Independently validated cross-culturally (Inderscience 2012). This is the canonical lineage requested.
- **Stage-specific image jobs — thumbnail.** Taboola native-stream guidelines: use bright, eye-catching images; avoid black-and-white (they blend into the feed). Job of the thumbnail = win attention/click within the content feed (https://s.yimg.com/cv/apiv2/adspecs/format/demos/542/NEW_Taboola_-_Native_Stream_Image_Guidelines__1708425977401.pdf). Outbrain best-practice guidance echoes image/headline optimization for CTR (https://www.outbrain.com/blog/outbrain-amplify-headline-image-best-practices/).
- **Stage-specific image jobs — advertorial/bridge (pre-sell).** ClickBank's bridge-page guide: the bridge/pre-sell page's job is to *warm up / pre-sell* leads before the pitch page and build know-like-trust — a distinct job from the ad (attention) and the sales page (conversion). Confirms the middle stage exists and has a different function (https://www.clickbank.com/blog/affiliate-bridge-page/). MGID's native-advertising guides frame pre-landers similarly (https://www.mgid.com/blog/native-advertising-guide).

## Notable Claims Requiring Cross-Reference
- Picture–text congruence effect was significant only for high need-for-cognition consumers (single experimental study) — moderator may limit generalizability; cross-reference before stating congruence works universally.
- Clickbait "certain features increase, others decrease" engagement is from one Facebook dataset (news posts, not native ads); direction may differ for paid native placements — corroborated in spirit by the Yahoo post-click paper but not identical context.
- ClickBank/MGID "bridge page warms leads / builds trust" claims are vendor-authored and not experimentally demonstrated in those sources; treat as practitioner consensus, validated conceptually by the academic congruence/credibility literature.

## Source Quality Assessment
- Journal of Interactive Marketing (picture–text congruence): **Tier 1** academic, 2009. Foundational theory (fluency) — not time-sensitive; age acceptable.
- Houston/Childers/Heckler, Journal of Consumer/Marketing Research: **Tier 1**, 1987. Seminal; age acceptable for foundational principle.
- NN/g Information Scent: **Tier 2** (authoritative UX research org), Feb 2020. Conceptual, not fast-moving; acceptable.
- Yahoo Gemini KDD 2015 paper: **Tier 1** peer-reviewed (ACM KDD), 2015. Platform mechanics may have evolved (>18 mo) — flag: use for the *principle* (post-click quality matters), not current Yahoo/platform specifics.
- PLOS ONE clickbait study: **Tier 1**, 2022. Solid; slightly dated but fine for a behavioral principle.
- Ismagilova et al. meta-analysis (JRCS): **Tier 1**, 2020. Strong, high-citation.
- Ohanian 1990/1991: **Tier 1**, canonical/foundational.
- Taboola Native Stream Guidelines: **Tier 3** vendor (official spec), ~2024. Current; authoritative for that platform's creative specs.
- Outbrain / MGID / ClickBank blogs: **Tier 3** vendor/promotional. Useful for stage-job framing; corroborate substantive claims with Tier 1.

## Gaps & Unanswered Questions
- No single source directly tests all three funnel stages together — the "three distinct jobs" framework is assembled from stage-by-stage evidence, not one integrated study. Worth stating as synthesis, not a single citation.
- "Bounded curiosity" (curiosity that pre-qualifies rather than maximizes) is conceptually supported (Yahoo post-click penalty + asymmetric clickbait effects) but lacks a source using that exact term measuring pre-qualification quality — a labeled gap.
- Limited peer-reviewed evidence specific to *advertorial hero image* function; the middle-stage image job rests mainly on vendor guidance + transferred congruence/fluency theory.
- Native-ad-specific (vs. search/PPC) message-match experiments are thin in Tier 1 literature; most congruence research is search/display/web.
